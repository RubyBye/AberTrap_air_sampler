# Prepare paddles for aerosol capture

## Wash paddles
To remove any grease/contamination aquired during cutting  of perspex sheet

**In sterile PCR cabinet**
1. Detach the paddles from the perspex sheet (making sure to remove both the central pieces from the slots) and drop into a bowl of soapy water
2. Rub gently (with sterile gloves) then rinse in a bowl of sterile water
3. Lay out to dry overnight on paper towels
4. Next day once dry, before greasing, zap with the hood's UV for 15 mins

## Coat with grease
**In sterile flow hood with external extraction**
1. Prepare Vaseline - hexane suspension (50 mg/ml) in a small sterile glass vessel eg 10 ml flask.

*note* Reserve a pot of Vaseline exclusively for this purpose and only open in sterile flow hood.
*note* The hood must be externally venting to safely remove the Hexane fumes during drying. 

2. Mix for approx 15 mins to allow the Vaseline to fully suspend.
3. Using a pair of sterile forceps, dip each paddle into the Vaseline - Hexane suspension. 
4. Place onto a wire rack (still under a sterile flow) to allow evaporation of the Hexane (5 mins), leaving a thin film of Vaseline covering the entire paddle surface.

## Package
1. Fit each greased paddle into the slot on an AberTrap arm. Use a pair of sterile forceps through the donut slots to manipulate.

*note* The curved upper corner of the paddle should face outward of when fitted into the arm. During operation, the direction of AberTrap rotation is clockwise, therefore by fitting the paddle in a known orientation the capture face can be easily determined.

2. Push a 2 ml screwcap centrifuge tube (without lid) over each paddle, where it securely locks into position on the arm. This shroud prevents contamination of the paddle prior to sampling, which is of utmost importance when conducting eDNA analyses.
3. Package the loaded arms into a box ready for transport to the experimental location. Do not remove the shroud from the tube until the arm is fitted to an AberTrap and experiment starts.

*long term storage* if paddles are not to be used immediately, seal into sterile 2ml tube and store double bagged. When needed, fit into arms under sterile conditions as above.

## Control 
To verify that paddles were successfully kept free of contamination, retain both:
- a greased paddle prior to loading onto the arms. Store in one of the sterile 2 ml microcentrifuge tubes used as shrouds.
- a pair of paddles fitted to the AberTrap and covered with shroud. Bring these to the sample site, but return to the lab unopened.

Process these negative controls alongside experimental samples. They should be completely free of DNA.
