# TE buffer recipe

## Ingredients

TE DNA storage buffer contains:
- 10 mM Tris (bring to pH 8.0 with HCl)
- 1 mM EDTA

How much to make: Each extraction uses 100 ul of TE buffer

## Method
[This site](https://gatescientific.com/technique-geeks-blog/f/recipes-for-te-tris-edta) has a good walkthrough for how to prepare TE buffer
