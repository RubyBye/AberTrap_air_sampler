# DNA extraction from AberTrap paddles
Salting out method 

## Before starting
Make fresh 70% ethanol. Store in icebox  
Set heat block to 60 ℃ 
Work entirely in PCR cabinet, except for bead beating and incubation steps

## Method  
- Remove aerosol loaded paddles (stored in 2 ml screwcap microcentrifuge tube) from freezer
   
### Macerate tissue
- Add 200 ul of sterile glass disruption beads * to each tube.
- Add salt lysis buffer (515 ul). 
- Beat 5 min using Disruptor Genie.

### Lyse cells
- Add SDS (57 ul = 2% final conc). Vortex briefly to mix. Pulse spin to get everything to bottom of tube
- Transfer tubes to heat block (60 ℃ x 60 min). Vortex every 15 mins incubation.
- Pulse spin (13,000 rpm) to draw down beads. Remove and discard paddles using sterile forceps.
   
### Precipitate proteins
- Add saturated NaCl (400 ul, 0.7x volumes). Vortex. The proteins will precipitate, the DNA remains in solution. 
- Spin down (13,000 rpm x 5 min) to pellet the proteins.
- Transfer supernatant (800 ul, containing the DNA) to a new 1.5ml tube.
- Repeat spindown
- Transfer the supernatant (750 ul) to a new 1.5 ml tube (this is the final tube so label fully)

### Precipitate DNA
- Add room temp isopropanol (~525 ul, 0.7x volumes)  to precipitate DNA, with glycol blue (1.5 ul). Vortex briefly. 
- Leave at room temp x 5 min. 
- Spin (13,000 rpm x 10 min) to pellet the DNA.
- Pour off the supernatant, taking care to retain the DNA pellet (which will be blue from glyco-blue). 

### Wash
- Add fresh, cold 70% ethanol (500 ul). Vortex briefly.
- Pulse spin to re-attach the pellet.
- Carefully pour off the ethanol, retaining the pellet.
- Repeat wash once more. Upturn the tube onto a sterile tissue to drain.
- To evaporate residual ethanol, leave with lid open on 60℃ heat block x 10 min.
- Re-dissolve DNA in [TE buffer](TE_buffer_recipe.md) (100 µl). 

## Buffers & Reagents:
- Salt lysis buffer: Tris 10 mM, NaCl 400 mM, EDTA 2 mM.
- SDS: 20% w/v
- NaCl: 5 M
- Mol grade isopropanol
- [Glyco Blue coprecipitant](https://www.thermofisher.com/order/catalog/product/AM9516) (optional)
- Mol grade ethanol
- [TE buffer](TE_buffer_recipe.md): 1 mM Tris [pH 8.0], 1 mM EDTA

* Glass disruption beads: [RPI Disruption beads 0.5mm for yeast/fungi](https://www.melford.co.uk/products/laboratory-supplies/homogenizers/disruption-beads-for-yeast-fungi-0-5mm-glass-2-5-g-cc-454-gm.html). Measure out a fixed volume of these beads using a 1.5ml tube marked at 200 ul as a measure. Pour the beads into the tube containing the paddle.
