# CTAB buffer recipe

## Ingredients
CTAB DNA extraction buffer contains:
- 100 mM Tris-HCl (pH 8.0)
- 1.4 M NaCl
- 20 mM EDTA
- 2% hexadecyltrimethylammonium bromide (CTAB).

How much to make: Each extraction uses 1ml of CTAB buffer 

## Sterilisation
- sterilise by autoclaving 15 minutes at 121 degrees celsius
