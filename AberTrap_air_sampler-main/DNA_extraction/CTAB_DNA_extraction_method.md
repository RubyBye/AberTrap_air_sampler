# DNA extraction from AberTrap paddles
CTAB method 

## Before starting
Make fresh 70% ethanol. Store in icebox.  

Set heat block to 60 ℃ 

## Method  
- Remove aerosol loaded paddles (stored in 2 ml screwcap microcentrifuge tube) from freezer
   
**In sterile flow cabinet:**

- Add 200 ul of sterile 200 um glass beads to each tube.
- Add 1ml [CTAB buffer](CTAB_buffer_recipe.md). 
- Beat 2 min using Disruptor Genie.
- Transfer tubes to heat block (60 ℃ x 60 min). Vortex 3x during incubation.
- Spin (13,000 rpm x 1 min) to draw down beads. Remove and discard paddles using sterile forceps.
   
**In fume hood:**

- Add 500ul Phenol/chloroform. Vortex until emulsion forms (~ 5 sec). Spin (13,000 rpm x 2 min) to separate phases. 
- Transfer the upper aqueous phase (900 ul, containing the DNA) into a new 2 ml tube.
- Add 500 ul chloroform/isoamyl alcohol (24:1 v/v). Vortex 5 sec. Spin (13,000 rpm x 2 min) to separate phases. 
- Transfer the upper aqueous phase (800 ul, containing the DNA) to a new 1.5 ml tube (this is the final tube so label fully). 
      
**In sterile flow cabinet:** 

- Add 0.7x volume (~560 ul) RT isopropanol to precipitate DNA, with 1.5 ul glycol blue. Vortex briefly. Leave at RT x 5 min. 
- Spin (13,000 rpm x 10 min) to pellet the DNA.
- Pour off the supernatant, taking care to retain the DNA pellet (which will be blue from glyco-blue). 
- Wash:
   - add fresh, cold 70% ethanol (500 ul).
   - Vortex briefly.
   - Pulse spin to re-attach the pellet.
   - Carefully pour off the ethanol, retaining the pellet.
  
- Repeat wash once more.
- Upturn the tube onto a sterile tissue to drain.
- To evaporate residual ethanol, leave with lid open on heat block for 10 min.
- Re-dissolve DNA in [TE buffer](TE_buffer_recipe.md) (100 µl).
